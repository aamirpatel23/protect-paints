Our Story

Pioneering Water-Based Paints in India

In a market traditionally dominated by oil-based paints, Aamir Paints (now Protect Paints) revolutionised the industry — bringing eco-friendly, high-performance alternatives to every Indian home.

Founded in 1996 by Mr. Siraj Patel in Palghar, Maharashtra. Today we manufacture exterior emulsions, interior sheens, primers, distempers and damp-proof defender coatings.
