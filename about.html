Since 1996

The origin of PROTECT Paints

PROTECT Paints, originally established as Aamir Paints in 1996 by Mr. Siraj Patel, began its journey in industrial paints, epoxy paints and primers. The company quickly grew into a trusted name in the construction and advertising sectors.

In a market traditionally dominated by oil-based paints, Aamir Paints revolutionised the industry by introducing high-quality water-based alternatives. Our pioneering product, Protect High Build Water Thinnable Enamel Paint, became a benchmark in quality and innovation.

Today, PROTECT Paints continues to lead the industry, offering innovative solutions and exceptional customer satisfaction — with exterior emulsions, interior sheens, primers, distempers and protective coatings for Indian conditions.

How We Work

Built for Indian conditions

Every formulation is tested for humidity, monsoon weather and high temperatures typical across India.

We prioritise water-based chemistry for lower odour, faster drying and a healthier indoor environment — without compromising durability.
