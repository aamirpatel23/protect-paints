Our Story

Pioneering Water-Based Paints in India

In a market dominated by oil-based paints, Aamir Paints (now Protect Paints) revolutionised the industry — bringing eco-friendly, high-performance alternatives to every Indian home.

Founded in 1996 by Mr. Siraj Patel, the company began with industrial oil paints, epoxy paints and primers. When quality of traditional solvents declined, we developed Protect High Build Water Thinnable Paint — a benchmark that still guides our innovation today.
