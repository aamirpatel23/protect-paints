Since 1996

From industrial coatings to everyday homes

Founded by Mr. Siraj Patel in Palghar, Maharashtra, the company began with industrial oil paints, epoxy paints and primers.

When the quality of traditional solvents declined, we developed Protect High Build Water Thinnable Paint — a benchmark that still guides our innovation today.

Today Protect Paints manufactures exterior emulsions, interior sheens, primers, distempers and damp-proof defender coatings for the modern Indian climate.

How We Work

Built for Indian conditions

Every formulation is tested for humidity, monsoon weather and high temperatures typical across India.

We prioritise water-based chemistry for lower odour, faster drying and a healthier indoor environment — without compromising durability.

Our factory in Khairepada Village (East of Palghar District) employs local talent and supplies depots in Pune, Nashik, Nagpur and partner distributors nationwide.
